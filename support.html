Milexplor

Light up every road you travel

Milexplor records your drives, walks, and bike rides, counts your miles, and lights up every road you travel on a map. Your trips stay on your iPhone.

Questions

How do I record a trip?

Tap Start, pick Drive, Walk, or Cycle, then tap Start. When you are done, press and hold End. Please start your trip before you drive, and do not use your phone while driving.

Does it keep recording when my phone is locked?

Yes. A trip you started keeps recording when the screen is locked or you switch apps. iOS shows a blue location indicator while it records. Recording stops when you end the trip.

Why "While Using the App" and not "Always"?

Milexplor only records trips you start yourself, so it never needs "Always". "While Using the App" is enough, even with the phone locked during a trip.

My trip is missing part of the route.

Check that Location is set to "While Using the App" and that Precise Location is on: iOS Settings, Milexplor, Location. GPS can also drop in tunnels and parking garages. The app shows "Looking for GPS" and keeps the trip going.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Trips record offline. The map pictures load again when you are back online.

How do I move my trips to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone: Settings, Back up now, and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone: install Milexplor, then Settings, Restore from backup, and pick that file. Restoring replaces the trips on the new iPhone.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does it use a lot of battery?

GPS runs only during a trip you start. Long drives use more battery, so plug in your phone for road trips.
