Milexplor

Privacy Policy

Short version: your trips, location, and photos stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who we are

Milexplor is an iPhone app made by Chinmay Laghate, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the maker of Milexplor. Questions? Email chinmaylaghate@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you create in Milexplor is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We have no server, and we never receive a copy. This includes:

  • your trips: routes, miles, times, speeds, titles, notes, and place names
  • your explored map and badges
  • photos you add to trips
  • your settings, like miles or kilometers

Location

  • Milexplor asks for location "While Using the App" only. It never asks for "Always".
  • It records your location only during a trip that you start yourself, to draw your route and count your miles. When you end the trip, recording stops.
  • If you lock your phone during a trip, recording continues so your route is complete. iOS shows a blue location indicator while this happens.
  • The map also shows your current spot (the blue dot) while you use the app. This stays on your phone.
  • Place names: when a trip ends, the app asks Apple's built-in iOS geocoding service for the names of the start and end places (for example "Danville to Napa"). Only those two points are sent, to Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies. If you are offline, this step is skipped.

Photos

  • Milexplor only sees the photos you pick. It asks for photo access only when you tap Add photo.
  • Picked photos are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.
  • If a photo has a location saved in it, the app uses that to show a small pin on your trip map.

The map (Google Maps)

Milexplor uses the Google Maps SDK for iOS to draw the map. To show it, your iPhone downloads map images from Google for the area on screen. Your trips, routes, and photos are not sent to Google.

Google's SDK collects some technical data on its own, such as crash and performance information, how the map is used, and a device identifier. Google says it does not use this data for tracking. Google's privacy policy applies to this data.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a crash reporting service, so we can fix the problem.

A crash report contains: what went wrong and where in the code, the app version, your iPhone model and iOS version, and which screens were open just before. The app also counts app launches and crashes, so we know how stable it is.

A crash report never contains: your location or routes, trip titles, notes, place names, photos, file names, your name, your email, your device name, or your IP address. Every report is cleaned on your iPhone before it is sent, and we ask Sentry not to store IP addresses. Crash reports are not linked to you, and Sentry deletes them automatically after a limited time.

Backups and sharing

  • Backup: a backup file is made only when you tap Back up now. You choose where to save it, for example iCloud Drive or Files. Whatever service you save it to has its own privacy terms.
  • Share cards: a trip or map picture is made only when you tap Share, and it goes only to the app you pick.

What we never do

  • No account or login
  • No ads, no advertising IDs, no tracking across apps or websites
  • No analytics of how you use the app
  • We never sell or share your data

Your control

  • Delete a trip in the app, and it is gone from your iPhone.
  • Delete the app, and all its data is removed from your iPhone. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them.
  • Turn location or photo access off any time in iOS Settings, Milexplor.

Children

Milexplor is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
