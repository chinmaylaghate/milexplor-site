How it works

Just tap Start

Three steps. No setup, no account.

1

Tap Start and pick Drive, Walk, or Cycle

The Start button sits in the tab bar, so it is one tap away from any screen. Pick how you are moving and tap Start trip.

  • Drive for highways and back roads
  • Walk for sidewalks and trails
  • Cycle for streets and climbs
  • Your location is used only while a trip is recording
2

Put your phone away

Lock your phone and put it in your pocket or a mount. Milexplor keeps drawing your route in the background until you end the trip.

  • Live miles, time, and speed when you check in
  • Pause for a coffee stop, then pick up where you left off
  • Ending takes a press and hold, so a bump never stops your trip
3

Press and hold End

When you arrive, press and hold End. Your trip story is ready right away, and the fog on your map clears along every new road.

  • Your full route on the map, with distance, time, and average speed
  • New roads discovered, like 6.9 new miles on a 15.9 mile trip
  • Any badges you unlocked along the way
  • Add photos and notes, share a card, or export a GPX file

Please start your trip before you drive, and never use your phone while driving.
